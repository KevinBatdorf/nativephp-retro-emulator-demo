Bundled homebrew — credits & licenses
======================================

The games bundled with this demo are freely-licensed homebrew, included to
demonstrate the emulator. Credit and license for each below.

GPL source offer (uCity)
------------------------

uCity's code is licensed GPLv3-or-later (some files are 2-clause BSD, e.g. GBT
Player); its graphics and music are CC BY-SA 4.0. The ROM bundled here is the
author's unmodified v1.3 release binary:

    ucity.gbc   131072 bytes
    sha256      9422ee2ca7b7ea1d46b58b2a429fff3f354dfd3e732dee1e7ae6220f148ce6e0

The complete corresponding source for that exact version is the v1.3 tag of the
author's repository, available at no charge from:

    https://codeberg.org/SkyLyrac/ucity        (canonical, tag v1.3)
    https://github.com/AntonioND/ucity         (mirror, tag v1.3)

Not modified by this project. If you redistribute this app, that source offer
has to travel with it — GPLv3 §6(d) permits pointing at the author's server
provided these directions stay next to the binary.
